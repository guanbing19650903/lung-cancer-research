\textbf{Re: Submission of Review Article entitled "Resistance Mechanisms to Targeted Therapy in Lung Cancer: EGFR/ALK/ROS1 Resistance Mechanisms and Overcoming Strategies"}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript for consideration as a Review Article in the Journal of Thoracic Oncology.

\textbf{Why this manuscript is appropriate for JTO:}

Lung cancer remains the leading cause of cancer-related mortality worldwide, and targeted therapy has revolutionized the treatment landscape for patients with driver mutations. However, acquired resistance inevitably develops, representing a major clinical challenge. Our review provides:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Comprehensive and Up-to-Date Coverage}: We systematically summarize resistance mechanisms to EGFR, ALK, and ROS1 targeted therapies, incorporating the latest research from 2024-2026, including the recent comprehensive functional atlas of ALK kinase domain variants (Oh et al., Genome Biol 2026).
    
    \item \textbf{Clinical Relevance}: The review includes practical treatment algorithms and resistance mechanism-based stratification strategies, directly applicable to clinical decision-making.
    
    \item \textbf{Novel Insights}: We integrate recent findings on receptor tyrosine kinase fusion-mediated resistance (Xia et al., JTO 2025), MET-TKI combination therapy meta-analysis (Hu et al., BMC Cancer 2025), and next-generation inhibitors (BLU-945, Zotizalkib).
    
    \item \textbf{Alignment with JTO Scope}: This review directly addresses JTO's focus on translational and clinical research in thoracic oncology.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Key Messages:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Resistance mechanisms are heterogeneous and dynamically evolving
    \item Repeat molecular testing at disease progression is essential
    \item Combination therapy based on resistance mechanisms is the future direction
    \item Next-generation inhibitors show promise
\end{itemize}
